Welcome to the Inuit Deaf & Hard of Hearing Youth Space

A visual-first community tailored for Northern youth. Explore Inuit Sign Language (ISL), learn Inuktitut syllabics through interactive graphics, watch captioned storytelling, and connect with peers across Inuit Nunangat.

ᐃᓕᓐᓂᐊᕐᓂᖅ

Inuit Sign Language (ISL)

Discover traditional gesture systems, community sign practices, and standardized visual vocabulary representing Arctic wildlife, weather, and daily life.

ᐃᓄᒃᑎᑐᑦ ᖃᓕᐅᔮᖅᐸᐃᑦ

Inuktitut Syllabics

Pair Inuktitut syllabic characters (ᐃ, ᐠ, ᑕ, ᑭ) with sign illustrations and English translations designed for visual learners.

Deaf Youth Connection

Share stories, participate in video art showcases, connect with Elders who know traditional gesturing, and join accessible youth gatherings.
